## [1.1.19] - 2026-10-07

### Fixed
- Luma category toolbar: the grid and list mode buttons have a #D4D4D4 border with #525252 icons (were #D1D5DB and #6B7280), the active mode is teal, and the Show, Sort By and per page labels are #525252 (were #6B7280).
- Luma layered navigation: the filter block, Shop By and option dividers, and the phone filter button use the #E5E5E5 border (were #E5E7EB and #CCCCCC).
- Luma swatches: borders are #D4D4D4 (were #D1D5DB), text swatches are #171717 (were #474747), hover adds a #525252 outline, and the selected swatch has a 2px teal outline.
- Luma home sliders, testimonial cards, product custom options, the header bottom line and the price alert and order attachment trust rows use the #E5E5E5 and #D4D4D4 borders (were #E5E7EB, #F3F4F6, #D1D5DB, #CCCCCC and #F0F0F0).
- Luma cart: the summary, totals, item table, header cells, item rows and discount block borders are #E5E5E5 (were #E5E7EB and #CCCCCC), and the empty cart heading is #171717 (was #1F2937).
- Luma mega menu and phone navigation drawer: dropdown and drawer dividers are #E5E5E5 (were #EEEEEE, #D1D1D1 and #E5E7EB), and the submenu, Account and Settings links are #171717 (were #575757) and secondary text #525252 (was #4B5563 and #6B7280).
- Luma footer: the newsletter and copyright dividers are white at 10% on the dark footer (were #262626 and #A3A3A3).
